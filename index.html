SpeedEQ privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026 (updated the same day: tuning emails). Applies to the SpeedEQ Android app (dev.dreamz.speedeq).

Short version: SpeedEQ has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Nothing it reads about you leaves your phone.

What the app uses, and why

Data Why Where it goes
Precise location (GPS) To work out your speed, which picks the EQ setting. Read about once a second while SpeedEQ is switched on. Used in memory to compute speed, then discarded. Not stored, not sent anywhere.
Microphone (only if you run Smart Tune) To measure how loud road and wind noise is at different speeds, so the app can suggest EQ settings. Only sound levels per frequency band are kept, with speed, in a file on your phone. No audio is recorded or saved. Delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.
Which app is playing audio (only in optional per-app mode) So the EQ attaches to just your music app and the notification can name it. Used on your phone only.
Your settings (EQ tuning, vehicle profile, theme) So they're there next time. Stored on your phone. Not backed up by the app.

SpeedEQ does not collect your name, email, contacts, files, photos or device identifiers, and it contains no advertising or analytics code.

Purchases

The paid upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy; SpeedEQ only receives confirmation of whether this Google account owns the upgrade.

Optional per-app mode

Out of the box SpeedEQ adjusts all audio from your phone. If you turn on per-app mode, by granting one permission with the ADB 5555 app, Shizuku or a computer, SpeedEQ can read the system's list of active audio sessions on your phone so it can find the playing app. That list never leaves the phone.

Free trial

The 3-day trial is timed on your phone from when you installed the app. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Sharing

SpeedEQ shares no data with anyone. Something leaves the app only when you deliberately use one of these buttons:

  • Share my tuning hands your EQ numbers to the app you pick.
  • Send my tuning to the developer opens your own email app with a message addressed to the developer. It contains your EQ numbers, the app version, the vehicle profile, the name of your audio output (for example your stereo's Bluetooth name), and your phone's make, model and Android version, plus anything you type. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app. The developer uses these emails only to improve SpeedEQ's starting tuning, and deletes yours on request.

Children

SpeedEQ is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: use the contact email on SpeedEQ's Google Play listing.
